## Overview

Phantom's Mask is a atmospheric puzzle-exploration game created in 72 hours for the Global Game Jam 2026 at EPI. The game won 1st place, showcasing innovative mechanics and immersive storytelling.

**Theme**: The abandoned castle holds dark secrets...

## Story

You are an explorer who has stumbled upon a forgotten castle. Deep within its walls, you discover a cursed artifact — **The Phantom's Mask**. This mysterious object grants its wearer a unique power: the ability to shift between the mortal world and the Phantom's World.

But beware — the castle is guarded by a fearsome Golem, and every shadows hides a secret.

---

## Core Mechanic

Press **E** to wear the Phantom's Mask and cross into the Phantom's World.

### The Phantom's World
- **Invisibility**: Enemies cannot see you
- **Intangibility**: You cannot interact with the physical world
- **Time Limit**: You can only stay in this realm for a limited time before the mask's power exhausts

### The Physical World
- Full interaction with objects, doors, and puzzles
- Visible to enemies — careful traversal required

**Strategy**: Use the Phantom's World strategically to bypass the Golem guardian and solve puzzles that would otherwise be impossible.
